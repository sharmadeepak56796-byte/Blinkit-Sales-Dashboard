## Key Insights

### Sales Performance

* The business generated total sales of approximately **$1.20M** from **8,523 products**.
* Average sales per product reached **$141**, indicating a healthy revenue contribution across the inventory.

### Customer Satisfaction

* The average customer rating was **3.97/5**, reflecting generally positive customer feedback and product acceptance.

### Fat Content Analysis

* **Low Fat products** generated the highest sales, contributing over **$717K** in revenue.
* Customer demand appears to be shifting toward healthier product options compared to regular-fat products.

### Product Category Analysis

* **Fruits & Vegetables** emerged as the best-performing category with sales exceeding **$178K**.
* **Snack Foods** ranked second, generating more than **$175K** in sales.
* **Household products** secured the third position, contributing nearly **$136K**.
* These three categories collectively account for a significant portion of overall revenue.

### Outlet Size Analysis

* **Medium-sized outlets** delivered the highest sales performance, generating approximately **$508K**.
* Small outlets contributed around **$445K**, while high-sized outlets generated approximately **$249K**.
* Medium outlets appear to offer the optimal balance between operating scale and customer demand.

### Outlet Location Analysis

* **Tier 3 cities** produced the highest revenue at approximately **$472K**.
* Tier 2 locations generated around **$393K**, followed by Tier 1 locations with **$336K**.
* This suggests stronger market penetration and customer demand in Tier 3 regions.

### Outlet Type Analysis

* **Supermarket Type 1** dominated sales with nearly **$788K** in revenue.
* Grocery Stores generated approximately **$152K**, while Supermarket Type 2 and Type 3 contributed similar sales volumes.
* Supermarket Type 1 remains the most effective retail format for revenue generation.

### Outlet Establishment Analysis

* Outlets established in **2018** recorded the highest sales performance, generating more than **$204K**.
* Outlets established between **2014 and 2018** consistently outperformed many newer locations.
* Mature outlets demonstrate stronger customer retention and operational efficiency.

## Business Recommendations

* Expand inventory in high-performing categories such as Fruits & Vegetables and Snack Foods.
* Increase focus on Tier 3 markets, where demand and revenue generation are strongest.
* Replicate the Supermarket Type 1 model in future expansion strategies.
* Continue promoting low-fat product ranges to align with customer purchasing trends.
